About Iron Fit

We build stronger people in Aurangabad

Iron Fit pairs modern workout machines with expert, certified trainers. Whether you want to build strength, lose weight, gain size or simply feel better every day, you'll find the right environment, guidance and tools here.

Wooden flooring, full air-conditioning, a proper music system, plus sauna, steam and an ice bath for recovery. Doors open at 5 AM, Monday to Saturday, so you can train before work or after it.

Your first visit

What to expect on day one

New to the gym or new to Iron Fit? Here's exactly how your first visit goes.

01

Walk in, Mon–Sat

Drop in any day Mon–Sat, 5 AM to 10 PM. Say hello at the front desk and take a look around the floor.

02

Talk to a trainer

Share your goal, check your BMI and get a plan that fits your level, beginner or pro.

03

Start training

Pick your membership and get to work. Your trainer corrects your form as you go.

Pro tip: bring clean indoor shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Lockers and a changing room are waiting.
